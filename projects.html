ZUM-IT · ZUMConnectLive on the Infor Marketplace

One ordering portal for every client

Slow business software turned into simple online ordering. Each feature is built once, and every client turns on only what it needs.

  • I built the design system behind it: a new client gets its own colours and chosen features, not a new design.
